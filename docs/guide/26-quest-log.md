# 26. The Quest Log

The **Quest Log** teaches SEOcc by having you finish quests on your own website. A quest is complete when SEOcc can see the real work in your data: a connected property, a recorded change, a created brief. Finishing the campaign means your project is fully set up and you have already acted on your first wins.

Open **Quest Log** in the navigation. It is available in every plan.

## Chapters and ranks

The campaign has six chapters. Finishing a chapter earns its rank.

| Chapter | Rank | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundations | Navigator | Set up cron, your website, data connections, keywords and rank tracking ([chapter 4](04-first-steps.md)) |
| 2. Reading the Signals | Analyst | Read search performance and rankings, set up alerts, inspect a page in Google |
| 3. Finding Wins | Strategist | Work with opportunities, priority bands, results-page formats and competitors |
| 4. Taking Action | Operator | Write a content brief, turn wins into team tasks, ask the AI assistant |
| 5. Proving Impact | Specialist | Record changes, measure their impact and outcomes, schedule reports |
| 6. Mastery | SEO Lead | Site audit, page monitoring, Bing, Google Analytics, Core Web Vitals, AI tools (MCP), backlinks, local, and the Weekly Check-in |

Each quest explains why it matters, what you will learn, its steps, pro tips (💡), common mistakes (⚠️), roughly how long it takes and the guide chapter to read. **Show me** opens the right page and highlights the place to act with a small hint; press Escape or **Got it** to close it. When the AI assistant is set up, **Ask AI about this quest** opens it with a question about your current step; each question uses your assistant budget.

## How progress works

- **Foundations is live.** Its status always reflects your current settings for the selected website, so a step can go back to **Needs attention**. Your Navigator rank is kept; a "Needs attention" marker appears on Chapter 1 and on the Quest Log card instead.
- **Other quests stay done.** They are checked when you open the Quest Log or its card, and every hour in the background, so waiting steps such as "first report delivered" finish on their own. Once complete, a quest stays complete even if the data changes later.
- **Your own work counts.** Where SEOcc records who did something (a change, a brief, a competitor, an opportunity status), the quest looks for your own work. Learning quests count work on any project you can see in the workspace; progress is per person and per workspace.
- **Only what you can use is shown.** Quests for features turned off on this installation, or for work your role can't do, are left out. Chapter progress counts only the quests you see, so every plan can reach 100 %. When your license gains features, **New quests available** lists the additions; ranks you earned are kept.
- **Waiting steps** (⏳) depend on background work, such as the first import or a measured outcome. You don't need to wait on the page.

## Skipping and hiding

You can skip any quest outside Foundations; a skipped quest counts as resolved and you can bring it back from its page. Foundations steps can't be skipped, because they are your actual setup. Steps your role can't perform are marked **Handled by your administrator**: they keep their live status but don't count against your progress.

**Hide this card** on the dashboard or project overview hides the Quest Log card for you in this workspace. The Quest Log itself stays in the navigation, where **Show it again** brings the card back. There are no forced tours, pop-ups on sign-in, leaderboards or streaks.

## Paid work

Viewing the Quest Log never queues work, calls providers or spends money. Quests that involve paid DataForSEO work (keywords, rank tracking, backlinks, local) say so: review your budget before you start them. Rank tracking is only switched on by you, on the Rankings page.
