# SEO Control Center: user guide

SEO Control Center is self-hosted SEO software. It runs on your own web hosting, your data stays on your server, and it works on ordinary shared hosting (cPanel), with no special server needed.

It brings together:

- **Pages:** clicks, queries, rankings, audit issues, AI citations, sampled backlinks and opportunities for each stored URL, with possible cannibalization and internal link suggestions;
- **Google Analytics 4:** Organic Search sessions, key events and purchase revenue by landing page;
- **Core Web Vitals:** Google CrUX mobile LCP, INP and CLS field data when available;
- **Search performance** from Google Search Console (up to 16 months of history) and Bing Webmaster Tools, plus IndexNow submissions;
- **Keywords** with search volume, CPC and competition, and **keyword research**: the keywords you and your competitors rank for, keyword gaps and ideas;
- **Rank tracking** of your keywords in Google, daily or weekly, including whether an AI Overview appears and cites you;
- **Competitors**: visibility and keyword gaps;
- **Backlinks**: your link profile next to your competitors', referring domains, new and lost links, anchor texts and the link gap;
- **AI visibility**: Google AI Overviews and how often ChatGPT and Google's AI mention you and your competitors;
- **Local SEO**: Google Maps positions, a ranking grid across your city, and your Business Profile;
- **Site audit**: broken links, redirects and on-page problems on your own site;
- **Opportunities**: concrete, prioritised to-dos found in all of this data;
- **Reports** by e-mail, weekly or monthly;
- **Alerts and weekly digest:** ranking and traffic drops, new audit errors, lost strong links and budget warnings;
- **Local branches and reviews:** separate branch point checks and manual imports of review texts and owner replies;
- **Ask AI**: a chat with Claude or GPT about your data, inside the app;
- **AI access (MCP)**: let ChatGPT, Claude and other AI assistants read your SEO data.

## Getting help

When you contact support, please include:

- a **support bundle** (*System health → Download support bundle*). It contains health checks and recent errors, never passwords, keys or SEO data.
