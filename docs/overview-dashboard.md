## Improved prognosis (local, unreleased)

`ProjectOverview` reads up to 180 daily Google rows through the existing scoped reader, plus Clock's as-of date. Forecast history is separate from the displayed period. Google-only training avoids mixing partial Bing daily imports; combined headline totals still include Bing. `forecast-model.js` is pure and bounded, with no network access or dependencies. `prognosis.js` caches results per horizon within the card and retains the existing chart controls/CSP module loading.

Candidates: last observation, 28-day linear trend, 28-day mean, weekday means (56 days), weekday residuals plus damped trend (84 days; trend saturates over a 14-day timescale). Candidates require 14 observations, a 13-day span and 80% observed-date coverage; weekday models require four examples per weekday. A maximum of twelve weekly origins is scored at each horizon, with 80% target coverage per fold. Baseline selection uses at least four common origins, comparing mean absolute daily prediction error on identical dates. Otherwise the recent mean is explicitly unvalidated. Missing/future/invalid dates are not zero-filled; latest source data older than seven days suppresses forecasts, as does a missing latest metric.

CTR auxiliary candidates regress historical CTR on position or log impressions (56-day window; 42 eligible pairs and nontrivial predictor variance). Future input values use training-only damped trends, never held-out actuals. Eligible auxiliary candidates and the selected baseline are tested on shared origins; at least four and 5% lower error are required. The strongest relative improvement on that shared sample is selected. Aggregate position/CTR relationships can reflect query composition; they are not causal SEO effects.

The candidate click pipeline multiplies predicted impressions × predicted CTR for aligned dates. It is adopted only when its nested historical MAE is at least 5% lower than the old linear-click benchmark; otherwise independent linear-click predictions are retained and need not equal the impressions/CTR product. With fewer than four origins the compound forecast is explicitly unvalidated. Their displayed MAE is measured by rerunning the entire selection/input pipeline with each origin's training prefix, and compared with the previous independent linear-click benchmark on the same dates. Endpoint ranges use the empirical 80th percentile of absolute errors from eight or more endpoints. These are historical error envelopes, not calibrated probability intervals; overlapping folds and model selection can make reported validation optimistic. More inputs are not automatically better. No automatic GA4, backlink, audit, CrUX or opportunity uplift is assumed. Seasonal annual patterns and calibrated intervals remain unverified. A read-only comparison on 81 saved Northwind Goods Google dates found helpful position inputs at 7/14 days, harmful impression inputs and a worse compound click pipeline; rejection guards retain the click baseline. This is historical model selection evidence, not proof of future accuracy.

## Reading order

1. Search totals and GA4 organic outcomes, with dates and comparison eligibility.
2. Trends & outlook.
3. Full-width query/page movement.
4. Actions, ranking distribution and keyword demand in the first independent stack; landing-page outcomes, competitive/AI visibility, traffic risks and recent changes in the second.
5. Where configured, Local evidence follows recent changes. Mobile uses the same DOM order, first stack then second.

Cards show an actionable empty state or are hidden when their source is absent. A project with no evidence gets one starting-point card. Search and analytics can have different coverage, reporting timezones and attribution; their totals are not interchangeable.

## Computation and limitations

- Search comparisons use equal preceding periods. Movers require completed dimension imports for every date in both periods, including successfully imported empty days and recorded zero-impression dates (the importer needs no breakdown for these). Only query/page keys observed in both periods qualify; an absent key is not a measured zero. The bounded sample balances gains, losses and unchanged rows, taking the largest absolute click changes within each group. Up to 500 matched keys are read, with three gainers and three losers displayed separately. Percentage movement is unavailable when the preceding value is zero.
- CTR actions use observed query peers in the same position band (1–3, 4–10, 11–20). Targets need at least 200 impressions; each peer needs 50, and the peer sample needs five queries and 1,000 impressions. Targets below half the impression-weighted peer CTR qualify. Estimated additional clicks equal target impressions multiplied by the peer CTR gap. This is an assumption using the displayed period and sampled peers, not a forecast or an external CTR curve. The input is the top 500 queries by clicks. Saved opportunities retain their stored priority, evidence and detection date; unlike units are not combined into an invented score.
- Ranking buckets use the latest exact device/day snapshots. Not-found checks are separate from unchecked keywords. Bucket changes use only keywords checked on both dates; the full current distribution, shared sample and comparison dates are disclosed.
- GA4 supplies Organic Search sessions, key events and purchase revenue. Engagement is not stored and is not inferred. Comparable periods require complete imports without report-quality restrictions and compatible metadata. Landing-page evidence joins up to 100 saved GA4 pages with up to 500 Search Console pages by exact URL, requiring 100 impressions and ten sessions. Key events per session is not a user conversion or engagement rate and can exceed one. Rates and prioritization are withheld when GA4 or search coverage is incomplete. GA4 currency/timezone and source sampling are shown.
- Research uses completed, timestamped saved results, choosing the latest result per keyword/locale. Tracking matches the same locale. Monthly volume is a provider estimate; unknown volumes are counted separately. Competitor gaps and striking-distance queries keep source sample/date labels. Multiple ranking URLs are evidence to inspect, not proof of harmful cannibalization.
- Competitor visibility is the existing tracked-keyword model. AI Overview citation rates use AI-present results as their denominator. Assistant/domain histories and current-brand platform histories remain separate samples; changing prompts or samples does not establish a comparable trend.
- Backlink profile differences are net snapshot changes, separate from saved new/lost-link samples. Null provider fields remain unavailable. Completed site audits are read directly without mutating audit lifecycle state. Audit issues are crossed with saved search page metrics; indexing concerns retain the current property binding. CrUX records show device, collection/fetch dates and actual supplied field metrics. Missing coverage or missing metrics are not a pass/fail assessment. Rendered diagnostics are saved provider evidence only.
- Annotation comparisons show observed search movement before/after the change, excluding the change day and requiring complete coverage. They do not claim causation. Local evidence appears only for a configured location and uses persisted checks and reviews.

## Bounds and verification

Source bounds are explicit in the cards: 500 search keys, 5,000 query/page rows, 100 GA4 landing pages, 500 audit issues and 100 CrUX records per device. Repository queries retain project/workspace scope. No SQL is added to views or controllers, no schema migration or runtime library is introduced, and no credential is exposed.

Fixed-clock tests cover movers, bucket boundaries, observed CTR scoring, empty/partial/analytics-only projects, locale-aware research, CSP and absence of outbound requests. Database tests cover comparison coverage, missing-versus-zero semantics, balanced samples and rank cohort/tenant isolation. Visual verification uses a local, explicitly seeded fixture rather than production metrics.
